Our work on **ACE is published in Nature**, and featured on the cover of the 23 April 2026 issue! Our paper, [“Outplaying elite table tennis players with an autonomous robot”](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10338-5), shares how we brought high-speed perception, reinforcement learning, and robot control together to compete with elite human players.

Table tennis leaves very little time to see, decide, and move. Getting those pieces to work together, rally after rally, took years of research and a tremendous team effort. Seeing that work reach Nature is a special milestone.

Grateful to my colleagues and collaborators, and to the players who challenged ACE and helped us push it further. Proud to share this achievement with the whole team!
