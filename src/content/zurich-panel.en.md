**What makes a city a great place for robotics? Is it the universities? The companies? The talent? Or simply the fact that you can go skiing after spending your week teaching robots how to move?**

I recently had the pleasure of joining the panel **“Why Zurich Is Built for Robotics,”** bringing together voices from research, industry, startups, and investment to discuss what makes Zurich’s robotics ecosystem special, what could be improved, and how we can attract and keep talented people, especially women in robotics.

The discussion was moderated by **Kateryna Portmann** (ANYbotics, Women in Robotics), alongside an inspiring group of panelists: **Prof. Dr. Yulia Sandamirskaya** (ZHAW), **[Maria Stamatopoulou](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mariastamatopoulou/)** (Laelaps AI), **Andie Zhang** (ABB Robotics), **Dr. Friederike Hoffmann** (b2venture), and myself, representing **SoftBank Robotics**.

Here are a few thoughts I shared during the discussion.

#### **Zurich: A Robotics Playground**

From drones in the sky to robots on the ground and underwater, Zurich has an incredibly diverse robotics ecosystem. With universities like ETH Zurich, startups like Gravis Robotics, and companies like ABB Robotics, the region brings research and industry together in one place.

At SoftBank Robotics, we’re already exploring collaborations with several local companies. With all the exciting developments happening here, I believe Zurich is becoming **one of Europe’s leading robotics hubs!**

#### **Attracting Talent Is One Thing. Keeping It Is Another.**

Zurich offers exciting projects, great career opportunities, and a high quality of life. But keeping talent means helping people grow and feel that they have a future here.

For women in robotics, visibility matters, but so do mentorship, career growth, and leadership opportunities.

**We don’t just need more women entering robotics. We need more women leading robotics.**

#### **From Research Labs to the Real World**

One thing that could make Zurich even stronger is giving robotics companies more opportunities to test their technologies in real environments.

Because making a robot work in a lab is one thing. Making it work in the real world is another!

**We already have the talent and technology. Let’s give robots more opportunities to prove themselves!**

#### **Come for the Robots. Stay for the Future!**

When asked what I would put on a billboard to attract robotics talent to Zurich, my answer was simple:

**“Come for the robots. Stay for the future!”**

Because Zurich is not just a great place to develop robots. It’s also a great place to build a career, meet inspiring people, and enjoy life.

#### **Final Thoughts**

What I enjoyed most about this panel was hearing different perspectives from research, industry, startups, and investment.

Zurich already has a lot to offer, but there is always room for more collaboration, more opportunities, and perhaps a few crazy dreams!

A big thank you to **Greater Zurich Area, Women in Robotics Switzerland, Kateryna Portmann, and my fellow panelists** for the great discussion!
