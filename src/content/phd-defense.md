On May 27, 2014, I successfully defended my PhD thesis on **Proximal Methods for Convex Minimization of φ-Divergences, with Applications to Computer Vision**.

This milestone marked the culmination of **three years and six months of research**, during which we developed and proposed novel optimization methods for **disparity estimation, image processing, and image denoising**.

It was a rewarding journey filled with scientific challenges, discoveries, and personal growth. I am deeply grateful to my colleagues, friends, and family for their continuous support.
