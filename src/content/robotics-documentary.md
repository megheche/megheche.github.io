I’m excited to be featured in **“Brilliant Minds: Robotics in the Greater Zurich Area,”** a 90-minute documentary produced by Greater Zurich Area, showcasing the people and innovations shaping the future of robotics in Switzerland.

The documentary brings together around 30 researchers, engineers, and entrepreneurs, offering a behind-the-scenes look at their work, their motivations, and the challenges of bringing robots into the real world.

In my segment, I share insights from my experience leading AI and robot learning research at Sony AI, particularly our work on **ACE, a robot trained to play table tennis at a competitive level**. We explore how robots learn, why seemingly simple human tasks remain difficult for machines, and the gap between impressive robotics demonstrations and real-world applications.

It’s a wonderful opportunity to highlight Switzerland’s growing robotics ecosystem and the people turning ambitious ideas into reality.
